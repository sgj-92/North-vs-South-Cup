Format & Scoring

🎾

Fast4 Scoring

First to 4 games wins a set, with a tiebreak at 3–3 played to 7.

⚡

Star Point

Golden point is played after the second deuce in every game.

🔟

Deciding Breaker

If a match reaches one set all, a match tiebreak to 10 decides the winner.

⏱️

Time Cap

Each tie is max one hour including breaks. Unfinished ties are recorded as a draw.

🔁

Rotating Pairs

Six a side, three courts per round. Partners rotate each round for as many rounds as time allows.
